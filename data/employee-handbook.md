# Acme Corp Employee Handbook

This handbook covers the core policies for all Acme Corp employees. It applies to full-time and part-time staff in every office. Contractors should read the Contractors section at the end.

## Working Hours

Our standard working week is 40 hours for full-time employees. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in your local time zone, and meetings should be scheduled inside that window whenever possible. Outside core hours you may organize your day as you like, as long as your manager agrees.

Overtime must be approved in advance by your manager. Approved overtime is paid at 1.5 times the normal hourly rate for hourly employees. Salaried employees receive time off in lieu instead of overtime pay.

## Remote Work

Acme Corp is a hybrid company. Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Teams choose their shared office days, which are usually Tuesday and Thursday.

Fully remote arrangements are possible for roles that do not need on-site equipment. They require written approval from both your manager and the People team. Fully remote employees must still attend the annual company offsite.

When working remotely you must use the company VPN for all access to internal systems. See the IT Security Policy for details.

### Home Office Stipend

Every employee receives a one-time home office stipend of $500 to buy a desk, chair, monitor or other equipment. The stipend must be claimed within 90 days of your start date through the expense system using expense code EXP-204.

## Leave and Time Off

### Paid Time Off

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid time off (PTO) per calendar year, plus public holidays. PTO accrues monthly at a little over 2 days per month. Up to 5 unused days can be carried over into the next year; any remaining days expire on March 31.

Request PTO in the HR portal at least 2 weeks in advance for absences longer than 3 days.

### Sick Leave

Sick leave is separate from PTO. Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year. A doctor's note is required only for absences longer than 3 consecutive working days.

### Parental Leave

Acme Corp offers 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave to all full-time employees who become parents through birth, adoption or foster placement. Parental leave can be taken in one block or split into up to two blocks, and it must be used within 12 months of the child's arrival.

Employees must have completed 6 months of service to be eligible. Notify the People team at least 8 weeks before your planned leave start date when possible.

### Bereavement Leave

Employees receive up to 5 paid days of bereavement leave after the death of an immediate family member, and up to 2 days for extended family.

## Expenses and Travel

Business expenses must be submitted within 30 days through the expense system with a receipt for any item above $25. Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to $75 per day. Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours; premium economy is allowed for longer flights.

Expenses for client entertainment need prior approval from a director and must use expense code EXP-310.

## Code of Conduct

All employees are expected to treat colleagues, customers and partners with respect. Harassment and discrimination of any kind are not tolerated. Concerns can be reported to your manager, the People team, or anonymously through the Ethics Hotline.

## Contractors

Contractors are not eligible for PTO, parental leave or the home office stipend. Contractors follow the working hours and security rules agreed in their contract and must complete the IT security training before receiving system access.
