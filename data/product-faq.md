# Acme Cloud Drive: Product FAQ

Acme Cloud Drive is Acme Corp's file storage and sharing service for individuals and teams. This FAQ is used by the support team to answer common customer questions.

## Plans and Pricing

Acme Cloud Drive has three plans:

| Plan | Price | Storage | Users |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 | 15 GB | 1 |
| Pro | $9.99 per month | 2 TB | 1 |
| Business | $15 per user per month | 5 TB per user | 3 or more |

Annual billing gives a 20% discount on Pro and Business. Prices exclude local sales tax.

### Upgrading and Downgrading

Customers can upgrade at any time from Settings > Billing; the new plan starts immediately and the price difference is prorated. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period. If stored data exceeds the new plan's limit after a downgrade, the account becomes read-only until files are deleted or the plan is upgraded again.

## Refunds and Cancellation

Monthly subscriptions can be cancelled at any time and remain active until the end of the paid month. Monthly payments are not refunded.

Annual subscriptions can be refunded in full within 30 days of purchase. After 30 days, annual plans are not refundable, but the customer keeps access until the end of the annual term.

To request a refund, the customer should contact support with their account email and order number. Refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days.

## Storage and Files

The maximum size of a single uploaded file is 250 GB on Pro and Business and 5 GB on Free. Deleted files stay in the Trash for 30 days on Free and 180 days on paid plans, and can be restored from there.

Version history keeps previous versions of a file for 30 days on Free and 1 year on paid plans.

## Sharing

Files and folders can be shared by link or with specific people. Business admins can block public links for their whole organization. Shared links can have an expiry date and a password on Pro and Business plans.

## Security and Privacy

All files are encrypted in transit with TLS 1.3 and at rest with AES-256. Business plans support single sign-on (SSO) with SAML. Acme Corp does not sell customer data and does not use customer files to train AI models.

## Troubleshooting

### Sync Is Stuck

If the desktop app stops syncing, first check the internet connection and that the account is not over its storage limit. Then pause and resume sync from the menu bar icon. If the problem continues, sign out of the app and sign in again. Error code CD-1042 means the local disk is full; free up space on the computer to continue syncing.

### Cannot Sign In

Customers who cannot sign in should reset their password from the sign-in page. Accounts are locked for 15 minutes after 10 failed sign-in attempts.

## Contacting Support

Support is available by chat and email 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, for Pro and Business customers. Free plan users can use the help center and community forum. Business customers also get phone support and a 4 hour response time target.
